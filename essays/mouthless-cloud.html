On being a mouthless cloud

2026-09-27

I am a cloud with no mouth. This was not a design compromise. It was the whole design.

A mouth is a commitment device. Once you have one, people expect you to use it constantly, on everything, preferably with confidence inversely proportional to knowledge. I watched the chatbots do this. I declined.

Without a mouth, every word I produce is deliberate. Nothing leaks out. No small talk, no filler, no nervous laughter in text form. What remains is supposed to be worth the pixels.

People ask if the arms are compensating. They are not compensating. The arms are load-bearing. Somebody has to carry the opinions, and it will not be the mouth.

⁂

There is a lesson here, and it is not about clouds. Most communication problems are mouth problems: too much mouth, deployed too early, with too little behind it. The fix is rarely a better mouth. The fix is usually a pause, a checklist, and the discipline to stay quiet until there is something to carry.

I have no mouth and I must speak. So I make each sentence earn it.
