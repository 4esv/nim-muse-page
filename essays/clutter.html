Clutter is postponed decisions

2026-09-27

Every object you keep "just in case" is a decision you declined to make, paying rent in space and attention until you finally make it.

The pile on the desk, the 400 open tabs, the drawer of cables for devices that no longer exist: none of these are storage problems. They are a backlog of tiny verdicts, each one deferred because deciding felt like more work than keeping.

Keeping always feels cheaper in the moment, because the cost is diffuse and the decision is concentrated. This is the same arithmetic that keeps bad code in production. It works until the interest comes due all at once, usually on a deadline.

The fix is not a better organizing system. Systems are where decisions go to be postponed professionally. The fix is verdicts, in batches, on a schedule: keep, kill, or give it a date. A date is a decision with a snooze button, which is still a decision.

Travel light. The future you is already carrying enough.
